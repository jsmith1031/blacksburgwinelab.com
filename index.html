Wine is a beautiful experiment.

A wine bar, bottle shop and kitchen in downtown Blacksburg.

223 Gilbert Street, Blacksburg

There's a reason we're called a lab and not a bar.

More than twenty years ago, John Boyer was putting up flyers around Virginia Tech for a geography of wine course he hoped a few students would take. Sixteen signed up. That class has since grown to 1,500 students a year, and John and Katie Pritchard have taught it from wine regions around the world.

In 2018 they opened the Wine Lab to bring that classroom downtown. You don't need to know anything about wine to sit down here. Tell us what you like, or what you've never tried, and we'll pour from there.
